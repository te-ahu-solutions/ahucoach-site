How it works

A conversation that gives context. A plan that gives shape.

  1. Tell AHU what's happening

    A small set of guided questions, one at a time. Pause whenever you need to and come back later.

  2. Receive your Action Plan

    AHU shapes what you share into a personal plan. You approve each part, so it fits your life.

  3. Return and take the next step

    Come back to your plan and choose what comes next. Inside AHU there's also a place to return to on the days in between.

Your Action Plan

It starts with the Mirror

Every plan opens and closes with words written for your situation, never a template. In between, it's clear and simple.

  • The MirrorA reflection of your situation, so you can see it more clearly. Honest, warm, and in plain words.
  • 1 PriorityThe one thing that deserves your attention now.
  • 1 Behavioural ShiftA small change that could make the next step easier.
  • 3 ActionsPractical, manageable steps you can take.
  • 1 AccountabilityA clear way to come back and check in with yourself.
Our approach

The heart of AHU

The values, practices and framework behind every plan. Inside AHU there's more to explore, with extra resources for members.

Our values

  • Pono

    Integrity & accountability

    Be honest with yourself, take responsibility for your words and actions, and have the courage to see what needs to change.

  • Manaakitanga

    Respect & compassion

    Meet yourself and others with dignity, understanding and compassion. Everyone has their own journey.

  • Rangatiratanga

    Self-leadership & agency

    Recognise your ability to shape your own path. Own the decisions and actions that move you forward.

  • Whakapapa

    Connection & belonging

    Understand the connections that shape who you are, so you can choose more consciously where you're heading.

Our practices

  • Asking the Hard QuestionsGetting past the surface to the assumptions and stories that keep you stuck.
  • Courageous ConversationsAn honest space to say what's really going on, without skipping the hard parts.
  • Vortex ExtractionStepping outside the emotional storm to see your situation with more calm.
  • Radical EmpathyAcknowledging what you're feeling and why it makes sense, before testing your conclusions.
  • Holding the MirrorReflecting back patterns in your choices and language, without judgement.
  • Power ReclamationShifting your focus to what's within your control, so you can act.

Our roots

Step through the waharoa, and put the load down.

AHU is a digital waharoa: a calm, private space to pause and think clearly before your next step. Restore your worth, reclaim your mana, and find steadiness.

It is inspired by Te Ao Māori and Ngā Kete o te Wānanga, the three baskets of knowledge Tāne brought down for all people. Your baskets are never empty. When you begin, you choose the areas you most want to strengthen.

Te Kete Aronui

Mindset, spirit and inner resilience.

Te Kete Tuatea

Structure, career and systems.

Te Kete Tuauri

Connection, family and aroha.

Why AHU

The clarity, confidence and agency to flourish on your own terms

AHU is an expression of the Te Ahu Solutions vision: people, organisations and whenua that flourish on their own terms, now and for generations to come. AHU brings that vision to each person.

"Most people already know more than they think. What they need is a safe place to pause and reflect, a different perspective, one clear priority, and something steady enough to carry them into the next real step."

Melanie Sweet, founder. AHU is what Melanie's own learning journey taught her, shared so others can find their footing too. It began with finding her pathway to her roots: the whakapapa that connects her to her tūpuna and whenua.

AHU supports you to think, with you in charge

AHU is

  • A self-guidance tool for times of change and uncertainty.
  • A private place to organise your thoughts and choose a next step.
  • Yours to pause and return to whenever it helps.

AHU is not

  • Therapy, counselling, or a medical or clinical service.
  • Legal or financial advice.
  • A substitute for professional care when you need it.

If you need support right now

AHU is not equipped to help in a crisis. Free call or text 1737 any time to talk with a trained counsellor, or call 111 in an emergency. Outside New Zealand, visit findahelpline.com.
