2. What AHU is, and isn't

AHU is a self-reflection tool to help you organise your thoughts during life transitions. It asks a set of guided questions and shapes your answers into a personalised Action Plan.

AHU does not provide medical, clinical, legal or financial advice. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition, and it isn't a substitute for therapy, counselling or professional care. Accessing professional care when you need it is your responsibility.

If anything you write suggests you may be at risk of harm, AHU will show you support options, and an administrator at Te Ahu Solutions Ltd will be alerted so we can meet our duty of care. AHU is not equipped to respond to a crisis. If you need support:

  • 1737, Need to Talk? Free call or text 1737, any time, to talk with a trained counsellor.

  • Outside New Zealand: find a free helpline in your country at findahelpline.com, or contact your local emergency services.

5. The AHU service

  • Guided questions: ten questions, most with set answer options, plus one required and one optional free-text question.
  • Action Plan: a seven-part plan (Opening, The Mirror, Priority, Behavioural Shift, Three Actions, Accountability and Closing). You approve each part.
  • Active plans: you can have up to three active plans at a time.
  • Check-in: a reminder to come back and reflect on your plan.
  • Export: you can download your plan as a PDF or email it to yourself.

Action Plans are generated using artificial intelligence (Claude, by Anthropic) from your answers. We design AHU's approach carefully, but we can't promise any plan will be accurate, complete or right for your circumstances. You remain responsible for your own decisions.
